# Stargate TTRPG — Player Guide

## Your Character

Your character has the following attributes:

- **Name & Rank** — Who you are and your title in the field
- **Level** — Your experience. Starts at 1 and increases over time
- **HP** — Your hit points. If they reach 0, you are out of action
- **Race** — Determines your combat multiplier (see below)

---

## Races

Your race gives you a multiplier that applies to all your actions in combat.

| Race | Multiplier |
|------|------------|
| Tau'ri | ×1.0 |
| Jaffa | ×1.25 |
| Tok'ra | ×1.5 |
| Goa'uld | ×2.0 |

Tok'ra characters also risk **Host Refusal** during unexpected outcomes (see below).

---

## Cards

Cards represent your abilities, weapons, and equipment. Each card has a **Power value**.

- **Abilities** — Skills and special techniques
- **Weapons** — Firearms, blades, and other armaments
- **Objects** — Equipment and tools

### Card Slots

You can carry a limited number of cards: **5 + your Level**.

A level 1 character has 6 slots. A level 3 character has 8 slots, and so on.

### Locked Cards

The GM can lock a card. A locked card uses a fixed value set by the GM rather than its normal value.

---

## Engaging an Action

When you take an action in combat or a tense situation:

1. Click **Engage Action** on your character sheet
2. Select the cards you want to use
3. Your **Total Power** is calculated: sum of selected card values × your race multiplier
4. Click **Submit** — the result is posted to chat

Choose your cards wisely. Higher value cards mean more power, but you may not always have the right cards available.

---

## Unexpected Outcome

After submitting an action, you can click **Roll Unexpected Outcome (d100)** in the chat. This introduces a random element to your action.

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1–5 | Catastrophic Failure — something went terribly wrong |
| 6–20 *(Tok'ra only)* | Host Refusal — the host refuses to cooperate |
| 6–95 | Normal Outcome |
| 96–100 | Amazing Success — everything went perfectly |

The GM decides how the outcome affects the narrative.
